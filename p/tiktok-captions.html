How to caption a video for TikTok

  1. Open Gluephs and drop in your video (MP4, MOV or WebM). The video stays in your browser; only the audio track is sent for transcription.
  2. Leave the language on auto-detect or pick it yourself. Gluephs writes the captions in seconds in English, Spanish, German, French, Portuguese, Italian, Dutch, Polish, Turkish, Russian, Ukrainian, Japanese, Korean, Chinese, Arabic and Hindi.
  3. Fix a word if needed and pick one of 30+ caption styles. Each word is highlighted as it is spoken.
  4. In the Style tab open Brand kit → Safe zone and choose the TikTok preset. The guide shows where the app interface can cover your text, and Gluephs warns you when a caption leaves the safe area.
  5. Export an MP4 (1080p, no watermark) or download the captions as SRT or VTT. Before your first download Gluephs asks for your email; there is no account or password.

What makes short-video captions work

  • Keep lines short: two lines at most, a few words at a time. One-word and two-word styles keep viewers reading in rhythm.
  • Use strong contrast between text and background. Outlines or a soft box keep captions readable on bright and busy footage.
  • Highlight the keyword of each phrase instead of colouring everything.
  • Stay consistent: use the same style across a series so your clips are recognisable.
  • Check the first seconds on a phone before you post. Captions are what many people read first.

Why the safe zone matters on TikTok

On TikTok the creator name, description and music line sit over the lower part of the video and the buttons along the right edge, with the top bar above. Captions that run into those areas get covered.

Gluephs has separate safe zone presets for Reels, TikTok and Shorts, and it is switched on by default for vertical 9:16 videos. Treat it as a rule of thumb: apps change their interface from time to time, so a quick look on your own phone is still worth it.

Frequently asked questions

Is it really free? Is there a watermark on TikTok videos?

Gluephs is free during the beta and never adds a watermark to your video. Before the first download we ask for your email address (no account, no password). There is a fair-use limit of 60 minutes of audio per person per day.

Is my video uploaded?

No. The video is processed in your browser. Only the audio track is sent to our transcription service to create the text; we do not store the audio or the transcript on our servers.

Which languages are supported?

English, Spanish, German, French, Portuguese, Italian, Dutch, Polish, Turkish, Russian, Ukrainian, Japanese, Korean, Chinese, Arabic and Hindi.

Can I edit the captions?

Yes. Edit the text word by word, change timing, merge or split lines, and switch styles without losing your edits.

Yes. Besides the MP4 with burned-in captions you can export SRT and VTT subtitle files.
