Open-source licences

CaptionRush builds on open-source software and fonts. Thank you to everyone who makes them.

Software shipped with the site

  • supabase-js — MIT. Licence · Source
  • mp4box.js (reads MP4) — BSD-3-Clause. Licence · Source
  • mp4-muxer (writes MP4) — MIT. Licence · Source
  • Hyphenation patterns (word breaks, from the TeX hyph-utf8 project) — various permissive licences, listed in vendor/hyphen/LICENSE. Source
  • FFmpeg (WebAssembly build, @ffmpeg/core 0.12.6) — GNU GPL v2 or later, built with libx264. Used only as a fallback to convert WebM to MP4 in the browser. The corresponding source code is available from github.com/ffmpegwasm/ffmpeg.wasm and ffmpeg.org; the GPL text and a written source offer ship with the files in vendor/ffmpeg/LICENSE and vendor/ffmpeg/SOURCE.txt. Your video is not sent anywhere for this.
  • Transformers.js and Whisper models — Apache-2.0 / MIT. Loaded only if you use the offline fallback, from jsDelivr and Hugging Face.

Fonts

The fonts offered in the editor come from Google Fonts and are published under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (a few, such as Roboto and Open Sans, under the Apache License 2.0). They are served from our own server (licence note). The copyright of each font belongs to its authors, listed at fonts.google.com.
