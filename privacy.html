Privacy policy

As of October 2026 · Deutsche Fassung

In short: Your video stays on your device. Only the audio is sent for transcription. We store your email address only if you enter it to download, and you get a newsletter only if you tick the box and confirm your address. We use no tracking cookies.

1. Controller

Jonas Treier, sole proprietorship web&meh, Hauptstrasse 84A, 5070 Frick, Switzerland
Email: contact@captionrush.com

We process personal data under the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and, where applicable (CaptionRush also targets people in the EU), the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

2. Your video and transcription

  • The video is not uploaded. Preview, editing and export run in your browser.
  • To create captions, your browser extracts the audio (only the parts with speech) and sends it in short pieces to our server at Hostpoint (Switzerland). From there it goes to Groq, Inc. (USA), which turns it into text with a speech-recognition model.
  • For proofreading (punctuation, capitalisation, names), for choosing keywords, emojis and zoom moments, and for the optional post text, we also send the transcribed text to Groq.
  • We store neither audio nor transcript on our servers and log no content. Groq acts as our processor under Groq's Data Processing Addendum (including EU Standard Contractual Clauses). How Groq handles data, and for how long, is set out in Groq's own terms (console.groq.com/docs/your-data).
  • Legal basis (GDPR): performance of the service you asked for (Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • If you save a project in your account (see section 4), the caption text is stored in the database. The video and the audio are never stored.

3. Email at download, and newsletter

  • During the beta we ask for your email address before the download. We store it with the time, your browser language and your newsletter choice in a database at Supabase (see section 4). Without the tick we use the address only to tell you about important changes to CaptionRush (for example the end of the beta). You get no advertising then.
  • Newsletter only with your tick and after confirmation (double opt-in): we send an email with a confirmation link, and the subscription counts only after you click it. As proof we store the consent text shown, the time of confirmation and a shortened, hashed form of the IP address. You can unsubscribe at any time, via the link in every email or by writing to us.
  • Your address is remembered on your device so you don't have to enter it again.
  • Legal basis (GDPR): consent for the newsletter (Art. 6(1)(a)), legitimate interest in service notices (point (f)). Giving the address for the download is voluntary but, during the beta, a condition for the download without watermark.

4. Account (optional)

If you sign in to save projects, we use Supabase (Supabase Inc., USA; database location Frankfurt, Germany) for the code sign-in and for your projects: captions, style settings and templates, but not the video. Supabase also keeps technically necessary sign-in data, including your IP address in logs. We send the codes through Hostpoint's mail server. You can have your account deleted at any time by emailing us; all projects are deleted with it. Legal basis (GDPR): performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)).

5. Hosting and server logs

CaptionRush is hosted by Hostpoint AG in Switzerland. When you visit, technically necessary access data (such as IP address, time, requested address, browser type) is processed in server logs, which are deleted according to the hosting provider's retention periods. Our server functions also use the IP address briefly to limit abuse (a cap on requests and audio minutes per hour). Legal basis (GDPR): legitimate interest in security and stability (Art. 6(1)(f)).

6. Embedded services

  • Fonts and software libraries are served from our own server. A normal visit loads nothing from Google or any other CDN.
  • Fallbacks: if our speech recognition is unreachable, your browser recognises speech itself. It then loads code from jsDelivr and the speech model from Hugging Face. If a local file is missing when converting to MP4, unpkg can serve as a substitute. These providers then see your IP address.
  • We use no tracking or advertising cookies and no analytics tools that build user profiles.

7. Storage on your device

CaptionRush stores settings, templates, an automatic backup of your project, your remembered email address and font files you add in your browser's storage (localStorage, IndexedDB). This is needed for the function and is not tracking. Your own fonts never leave your device. If you are signed in, your sign-in (token) is kept there too, and your templates are synced with your account at Supabase. You can delete everything in your browser settings; settings and remembered items then return to their defaults.

8. Transfers abroad

Groq (USA) and Supabase (USA) are based outside Switzerland and the EU/EEA. We transfer data to them under data processing agreements with standard contractual clauses or, where the providers are certified, under the Swiss-U.S. and EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. Your data can also pass through further countries on the internet.

9. Retention

We keep email addresses until you ask us to delete them or we shut the service down; newsletter proof (time of confirmation) is kept for as long as the newsletter exists and afterwards until possible claims expire. Account data and projects are deleted with the account. Unsubscribed addresses stay only on a block list so you get no more emails.

10. Your rights

You can ask for access to your data and for rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, or a copy in a common format (data portability). You can object to processing based on legitimate interests and withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future. Write to contact@captionrush.com. You may also complain to a data protection authority: in Switzerland the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC), in the EU the supervisory authority where you live.

11. Children

CaptionRush is for people aged 16 and over. Younger people may use the service only with a parent's consent and may not subscribe to the newsletter.

12. Changes

We update this policy when the service or the law changes. The version published here applies.
